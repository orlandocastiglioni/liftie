Today's Mountain Conditions

3/27/26

 Good morning skiers and boarders.  Happy Closing Day!

It’s currently 24 degrees with  clear skies at the mountain this morning.  Today will be sunny with a high near 55 degrees.

Here’s the playlist for today.

Marge’s Triple is now closed.  There is no “Easy Way Down” from Gentle Ben.  The Grand Canyon is still open.

The recommended route down from Ridge and Stump is “Lower Ridge” going down to the Gentle Ben restroom.  There are bare spots on the mountain so please ride with care.  With the hard freeze last night this should be the best corn skiing we’ve had but will take a little longer to soften.

We’d like to thank all of our guests for being so supportive through a challenging year.  Come on up and let’s kick this season out the door in style!

 

Closing day will be Friday, March 27.  Hope you can all make it.

Logan canyon is mostly bare and dry.

 

 

A reminder to anyone purchasing day passes in the future to please set up your accounts beforehand as we are moving to RFID tickets.  This will greatly expedite the process when you are purchasing your passes.  You can pick up your RFID cards at the Logan office as well.  Detailed instructions can be found here.
