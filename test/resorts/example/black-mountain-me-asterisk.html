Mountain Report

February 21st 6:00am
We realize we are repeating ourselves, but conditions really are outstanding. Jeff and John have rolled out the corduroy carpet and it’s going to be a great day to enjoy skiing and riding on 31 trails with machine groomed and packed powder conditions. There's still chances to discover hidden powder stashes on our ungroomed terrain. Today’s forecast is calling for mostly cloudy skies with temperatures getting into the mid 20s. We’ll see how that goes. Yesterday the “mostly cloudy sky” forecast resulted in a total bluebird day. As the Maine saying goes, “Don’t like the weather? Just wait a minute.”
Tickets today are just $30 with free snowshoeing, free nordic skiing and $5 uphill tickets sponsored by L.L.Bean.
School break is underway and we’re open all week from 9am-4pm for skiing and snowboarding and the tubing park will be running 12pm-4pm every day.
Today we welcome back Vertical Challenge, the funnest free race series around! Fun, games, prizes, free samples plus ski racing of course! Get all the info HERE
The Osekare Uphill Trail is open with Exit 2 being the preferred exit. The uphill trail is open 24/7. Exits 1,2,3, and 4 are open when the lifts are spinning. Exit 5 is always open.

This weekend the Last Run Pub welcomes back local favorites Kitty & JT. This Maine duo brings a wide range of genres, showcasing Kitty's captivating vocals paired with JT's expert guitar playing on Saturday from 2pm-5pm. Sunday Acoustic Sunshine, a Maine-based duo, will hit the pub for the first time. Since 2021, they've been bringing good vibes with covers from artists like Jack Johnson, Sublime, Grateful Dead, the Rolling Stones, & more. Both shows are all ages, and there’s no cover charge.

Uphill

Osekare Uphil Trail Note:
The Osekare Uphill trail is open! Exits 1, 2,3 & 4 are open during operational hours (9am-4pm Thursday - Sunday). Exit 2 is the preferred exit & uphillers may continue uphill from this exit on the side of the Allagash trail until they reach the summit patrol shack. Exit 5 is open 24/7 along with the following sidecountry trails & their tributaries: Headwaters, Saco & Bagaduce. Our full policy regarding uphill traffic & sidecountry trails can be found via the link below.
