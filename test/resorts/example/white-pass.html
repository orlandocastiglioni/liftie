HOURS, LIFTS & TRAIL MAPS

OPERATION PLAN:

During the winter season, White Pass is open daily including holidays (snow/weather conditions permitting). Base area lifts run 8:45am until 4pm daily. Day Lodge opens for breakfast at 7:15am; all other skier services are open from 8:00am until 4pm. 

Paradise Basin, High Camp, Basin Quad and Couloir Express close promptly at 3:15pm daily.

Our lifts serve 1402 acres of ski and snowboard terrain.
