Weather Forecast

Cloudy and cool this morning in Ellicottville, with temperatures starting around 23°F (-5°C). Some Snow in the forecast and warmer temperatures on the way.

Caution

Dawn to Dusk Uphill traffic is allowed with your annual $25 Uphill Traffic Pass. If you are skinning uphill with a group on an open ski slope please do so in single file. Please stay away from terrain where we are currently making snow or grooming and look out for our mountain operations crew on the hill.

Special Events & News

Thank you for an incredible 2025/26 Ski & Ride Season!

While the winter season has come to a close, we are so grateful to our membership, team, guests, and local community for making it such a special one.

With over 180 inches of natural snow, 113 days of lift-serviced skiing and riding, and countless memories made along the way, it was a season to remember. We already can’t wait for next winter, but the fun is far from over.

Be sure to mark your calendar for Bike Park Opening Day on May 23, 2026.
