MARCH 11


Temperatures cooled off overnight, bringing more winter-like conditions back to Schuss Mountain for the final KHQ Ski Night of the season. Tonight from 5p–9p, lift tickets are just $22 and rentals are $33.

The Pink, Yellow, and Purple Chairlifts and the Magic Carpet are running 9a–9p. The Low Rider Tow Rope opens at 3p and runs through 9p. The Red Lift will be opening mid-afternoon today following maintenance by our mountain crew.
