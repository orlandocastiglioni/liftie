Updated: Monday, November 2nd

That’s a wrap on the 2026 Fall Season.

What a season! From crisp mountain air and colorful foliage to unforgettable weekends on the mountain, fall at Mountain Creek was one for the books. A huge thank you to everyone who came out and made the most of everything the season had to offer.

Now, we’re shifting gears and setting our sights on winter! Colder temps, snow-covered trails, and plenty of mountain adventures are right around the corner. Who’s ready for winter? ❄️
