Written Report

Updated:
09:51am, January 05, 2026

Update, we will be closing the Eclipse triple at Noon. 

 

Happy Monday! It will be a great day to get some laps in today. Groomers are coming off the hill within the hour. All lifts and 17 trails are open! We are running the Eclipse Triple in addition to the Hornet. Platter and Tow Rope too! Less crowds as the work week starts up. We hope to see you soon! Check out our Learning Area the Platter, a great place to start! Also grab a great lunch in the Back Country Bar and Grill!

Trails

There will be 18 total trails open for skiing and riding today. The Hornet Double will give access to Shooting Star, Edelweiss, Lights Out, Last Chance, Morning Glory, and Blizzard Bailout. The Eclipse Triple will give access to Trillium, Pitcher, Hardhack, Hall's Meadow, and Eclipse. The Platter will also be open all day today for beginner skiers and riders.

Terrain Parks

The PLN Park and rope tow will be open at 9:00 AM and run until 4:00 PM. Thank you to all the skiers and riders who showed out for our NYE rail jam on December 31st. It was such an awesome event, and we can't wait to have everyone back for some more park events this season! Follow @tenney_parks on Instagram for some terrain park content!

Uphill Policy

Uphill travel is now available at Tenney Mountain. Before participating in any uphill travel, please familiarize yourself with our Uphill Policy. All uphill travel is only permitted during operational hours. Any uphill travel outside of operational hours is strictly prohibited. The first trip up can begin at 8:30 AM, and the last trip up can begin at 3:00 PM. All uphillers must travel on our designated uphill routes. You may choose to ski down any trail that is considered open by Ski Patrol. For Uphill New England Passholders, check-ins are located at the base of the Hornet Double.
