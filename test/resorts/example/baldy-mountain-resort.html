Lessons

Turn your first day on the slopes into a lifelong passion. Our ski and snowboard lessons will help you gain confidence and have fun, with friendly certified instructors.

We have lessons and rental packages for every age and ability, whether you’re on your first run or refining techniques from years in the powder. See you on the slopes!

Tickets & Passes

Bring the family and spend a day skiing or riding at Baldy Mountain Resort in the beautiful South Okanagan. We have high elevation, endless fun, and a variety of affordable ticket options to choose from.

If you’ve visited us before, don’t forget your RFID card. It’s required for lift access and can be reloaded for future visits.

Baldy Events

We’re proud to host a wide range of mountain and Okanagan events all year long. Surrounded by stunning natural beauty, Baldy is the ideal spot for your next celebration. Join us throughout the year for one of our many community events, or plan your own right here on the mountain.
