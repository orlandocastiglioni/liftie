Mountain Now Closed Until Summer Season Begins

Thanks for a fun season together, Washy community. Our operations have wrapped up, and the mountain is now fully closed. Prepare for next winter now with Early Bird Season Pass deals at the lowest price of the year.

While we transition into summer mode, crews are actively working with machinery around the mountain to prepare for ziplining, mountain biking and more activities. For everyone’s safety, please remain off the slopes and out of closed areas. We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to welcoming you back this summer.
