Good Morning skiers and riders! We are open today, Friday, February 2nd. Last night we received a nice dusting of snow on the mountain.

 

 

Lifts and Trails:

Lifts open at 9:00 am. We anticipate spinning the following lifts: Tecumseh Express, High Country T-Bar, Green Peak, Valley Run Quad, Lower Pasture Carpet, Lower Meadows and Exhibition. 

Higher Ground and Lower Bobby's are now open! 

 

We currently have 58 trails open across two mountain peaks, with 256 skiable acres!

 

Expect snowmaking to resume later today on Piano Man, Calvert's Crossing, and True Grit.

 

Conditions and Weather:

Today's temperatures will reach 25 degrees at the summit and 34 degrees at the base, accompanied by mostly cloudy skies.


Winds are expected to reach 14-20 mph from the north at the summit, and 10-16 mph at the base area. 

 

Thanks to recent snow, trails are consisting of soft corduroy with firmer yet carvable conditions on select trails. 

 

 

Nordic:

Nordic skiing is open, Click here to view the full Nordic Report!

 

The Nordic Center is open for retail daily --> Click here to visit the Nordic Center!

 

Terrain Parks:

Our main park on Utter Abandon is open! So far there are 5 rail and box features, with 2 large and 2 medium jumps. The build is continuing throughout the next week!

High Country Park: 2 tubes, 1 medium jump

Rock Island Park: 2 tap features, 3 rails, 1 small jump

Lower White Caps: 1 tube, 1 box, 1 rail

Boardercross Course: Variety of banked turns, rollers, jumps, and drops.

FunRun: Small banks, rollers, and tabletops.

 

Dining:

The following dining options are open today: Schwendi Hutte, Freestyle Lounge, Next Level Food Truck, T-Bars, and Marketplace. We have new and enhanced menus at these spots so stop in and fuel up!

 

Uphill Access:

Oblivion Route: Open Sun-Fri only (No Saturday or Holidays) from 7am-10am: Valley Run -> Upper Valley Run -> Oblivion. Please keep to climber's left of trail during ascent. Must descend by 10am.

 

High Country Route: Open: Main Street -> Ruthie's Run -> Tree Line. Uphill access every day during normal operating hours (lift-serviced hours)

 

Green Peak Route: Open: Leroy's Loop -> Bourbon Street -> Stillness -> South Street -> Chandler's Way

 

IF DESCENDING PRIOR TO CHAIRLIFT OPERATING HOURS, YOU MUST DESCEND THE SAME DESIGNATED ROUTE YOU ASCENDED

UPHILL RULES: Uphill access is permitted only to those who purchase a valid Uphill Trail Pass (reflective armband) and sign a Liability Release. Reflective armbands must be worn while ascending. No descending until lifts open on powder days. No dogs. Only skinning and touring are allowed on alpine trails, sorry, no foot traffic is permitted. 

 

*These restrictions and modifications are temporary. Once snowmaking is complete on Green Peak Route, we will revert to normal designated trails.
