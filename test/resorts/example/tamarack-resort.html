Good Morning Skiers and Riders!

This is Ryan bringing you the 7 am weather update for Friday, January 23rd, 2026.

It is currently 2 degrees at the base and 20 degrees up at the summit. NOAA is forecasting a mostly sunny day, with a high near 25 degrees. There will be mild wind from 5 to 8 mph out of the southwest.

The best turns today are going to be on freshly groomed runs from the summit to the base.

As always, stay safe out there and have fun!
