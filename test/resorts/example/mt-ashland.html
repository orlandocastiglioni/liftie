Mountain Report

Summer time baby! The Karen and Sid DeBoer Lodge is open for summer Fridays-Sundays until Sept. 2nd. Check out our operating schedule here!

There are a number of things to do on your local mountain playground this summer! Utilize our summer trail map to plan your adventure and stop by the lodge for a bite to eat or a refreshing beverage. Grab some ice cream and enjoy the mountain views! If you forgot your sunscreen or want some Mt. A summer merch, we’ve got you covered with our retail items.

 

Updated March 2, 2026 at 12:40 PM

Mt. Ashland’s Mountain Report is brought to you by Lithia Motors, through their Lithia4Kids program.

Mt. Ashland Ski Area Ski Area Operations are Paused

Pending Sufficient Snowfall

With just 14″ at our base and only warm temperatures and rain in the forecast, the Ski Area is forced to pause operations, until we get sufficient snowfall.

Lift tickets, rentals and lessons purchased for the upcoming days will be refunded so if you have purchased, please keep an eye on your email inbox. 

If your child is involved in the School & Youth Group Learn-to-Ski Program, our School Group Program Coordinator will be contacting your Volunteer Coordinators in the event dates need to be adjusted.

We’re back to snow dances.

Bavarian Night is still happening March 14th—snow or sunshine! We’re keeping our fireworks tradition and Bavarian festivities alive, even if Mt. Ashland Ski Area operations are paused. Join us in supporting Mt. Ashland Ski Patrol at their biggest fundraiser of the year. After a challenging snow season, this is the perfect opportunity to come together, reconnect, and celebrate our incredible mountain community. Visit our events page for full details—we can’t wait to see you there!
