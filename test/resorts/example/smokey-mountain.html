CURRENT SEASON INFORMATION

 

Smokey Mountain Ski Club is open for downhill fun annually from mid-December to April*
*pending snow level and extreme cold warnings

 

Lifts, canteen, and Pro Shop are open:

Fridays 6-9
Saturdays 10-5
Sundays 10-5

 

Toby's Place (bar) opens:
Friday at 6pm
Saturday and Sunday at 11am

 

Please refer to Smokey social media for announcements on closures due to weather (extreme cold, etc). You can also Contact Us before heading to our hill!

For up to date information on lifts, please refer to Smokey social media each open day.
