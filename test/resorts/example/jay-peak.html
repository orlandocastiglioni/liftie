Snow Report

Thursday March 5 at 7:58 AM

Corduroy & Sunny Skies

Upper Mountain: 9 a.m.–4 p.m.
Lower Mountain: 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m.

9 of 9 lifts are planned to run this morning.

No new snow overnight, but the grooming crew went to work while the rest of us slept, laying down fresh corduroy across the hill. With a season total holding at 354", the base remains deep and coverage continues to run wall-to-wall.

Expect a bit of a mixed sky today. The summits will spend some time in and out of the clouds while lower elevations see some filtered sunshine. Temperatures land in the mid-20s with light winds, making for a comfortable day to stack some laps.

Conditions start on the firmer side this morning before softening up as the day progresses. Groomers should be especially friendly early, and the trees still have plenty of personality if you're willing to poke around a little.

Looking ahead, clouds thicken tonight with a chance for a little fresh snow to drift through while most of us are off the hill. Nothing dramatic, but enough that we’ll be keeping an eye on it heading into Friday. And as always this time of year, March likes to keep a few surprises up its sleeve. Tim Kelley drops his next forecast tomorrow with a closer look at what the week ahead may have in store.

If you're already thinking about the weekend, the lineup is shaping up nicely. Real to Steel returns Saturday, bringing a mix of freeride lines and park precision to one of the East’s most unique competitions. The Gear Shop will also host another Demo Day ,and the live après lineup is back with Sleeveless Tease, Seth Yacovone, and a few familiar faces filling the Bullwheel Bar, Tower Bar, and Sis Boom Burgers.

Uphill Travel remains open to the summit and to the top of the Jet via designated routes.

All five Terrain Parks: Rusch, Shakedown, Rabbit, Gully, and Rabbit Hole are dialed and ready for laps.

The Nordic Center is fully open and freshly groomed, with classic and skate lanes winding through the snowy woods.

Conditions and operations status are never set in stone. As soon as we know, so will you, so keep it locked in to the Snow Report for all the latest updates.

Safety Note

Collision avoidance on the slopes is everyone's responsibility. These three tips will help to keep you and others safe on every run. 

  • Be Ready to slow down or avoid objects/other people at any time. 
  • Stay Alert to what's going on around you, especially at trail intersections or where glades connect into trails. 
  • Plan Ahead and ease up in slow skiing/riding zones, check uphill when merging onto trails, and give others plenty of room when passing. 

Slowing down in congested areas, being mindful of folks with different ability levels, and staying aware of your surroundings will help keep the slopes safe and enjoyable for all.
