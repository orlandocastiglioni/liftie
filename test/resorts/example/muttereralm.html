Muttereralm

Family. Experience. Nature.

More than just slogans await visitors at the Muttereralm skiing resort. Family. Experience. Nature. form a snow-white symbiosis on more than 15 kilometres of slopes with lengths between 700 and 4,200 metres. 
The Mutterer Alm Ski Pass is part of the SKI plus CITY Pass Stubai Innsbruck and provides access to a real paradise for all those who want to exchange the hustle and bustle of everyday life for nature-oriented pleasures. 

Highlights

  • Perfect skiing area for families, beginners, and skiers returning to the spot
  • Kinderland right at the Summit Station
  • Two toboggan runs
  • Snowpark

Nature-oriented pleasures

Beginners and skiers returning to the sport alike will feel at home on the downhill runs around the Summit Station, while well-trained skiers can explore the slopes to Mutters and Götzens. The Götzens run is particu-larly scenic.

On the former reserve track of the Olympic Games, a wonderful landscape experience is included. When skiing this slope, you can marvel at one of Austria’s most beautiful forest descents. 

The skiing resort in Mutters also offers impressive deep snow runs for freeriders. The youngest guests can enjoy magic carpets at the Kinderland near the Muttereralmbahn Summit Station or at the Nockspitzbahn Summit Station’s Kinderland. The fairy-tale forest of the local ski school is a special feature that makes the skiing holiday in Mutters particularly exciting. Two toboggan runs, a permanent fat- and snowbike track, as well as a cross-country skiing trails complete the Family. Experience. Nature. offer.

Pfriemesköpfl 1,887m

Equally loved by locals and guests as well as scenically charming, the 1,887 metre Pfriemesköpfl is the heart of the Muttereralm. 8-seat gondolas (Nockspitzbahn) take winter sports lovers quickly and comfortably from Götzens to the Pfriemesköpfl.
