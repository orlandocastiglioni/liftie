TODAY’S REPORT

1/31/26:

4PM: Thanks for a wonderful Saturday! See you tomorrow!

6 AM – It’s Saturday and it’s time to get out and enjoy all that Mother Nature has to offer! RLM has 12 trails of machine groomed terrain open to explore. Come get some fresh air and stretch those legs. 5 lifts will be turning today, including Grizzly Peak. Temps are expected to be mild with light winds- perfect for the fair-weather skiers out there.

Sign up for operations text alerts by texting: “RLMAlerts” to 844-928-4336.
