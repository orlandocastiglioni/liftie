The Best Terrain In Pennsylvania
Montage Mountain

27 of the best trails in PA and some of the best terrain in the East can be found here at Montage Mountain!  With 100% snowmaking, a great variety of Beginner, Intermediate, and Expert slopes, and conditions cared for by our team of pro groomers, you can be sure you’ll find a great time on the snow in Scranton, PA!

Thursday, January 22nd, 2026
The Snow Report

We’re over the hump and temps are out of the basement making for an awesome Thursday on the snow! Your luck, we added Ski-For-All $45 tickets and free rentals to Thursdays this season so you can ski or ride for less later in the week! Crews have been making snow around the mountain and adding to the already premium surface. You’ll notice some big piles in The Yard as we ready for a rebuild and refresh in both parks! Stay tuned starting tomorrow for updates as we work! Also today is College Day, save more as a student with your college ID, grab a ticket at the window for $35 and rentals are FREE all day. Don’t sleep on another W day on the hill. Lifts spin 9-9, get those legs warmed up for what looks like a deep weekend ahead! We’ll see ya out there!
