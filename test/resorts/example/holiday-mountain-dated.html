Holiday Mountain's Oktoberfest Beer Festival

October 24, 2026
Join us for the 3rd Annual Holiday Mountain Oktoberfest Beer Festival! *** 21+ years old only.
Come celebrate with us as we bring together the best of local craft beer, delicious food, and live music at Holiday Mountain. Whether you’re a beer enthusiast or just looking for a fun day out, there’s something for everyone!
What to Expect:
 Full cash bar, beer samples, slushies, ciders and more!
 Tasty food options to satisfy all cravings
 Live music to keep the party going
 A souvenir glass to take home with your Taster ticket
Chairlift rides to view fall foliage
Don’t miss out on the ultimate beer fest fall celebration! Invite your friends, mark your calendars, and get ready for an unforgettable day at Holiday Mountain.
Tickets available at: https://shop.skiholidaymtn.com/

Beach Bash Pond Skim

March 22, 2026

Join us for our 3rd annual pond skim at Holiday Mountain!

This year our Beach Bash Pond Skim will have a different theme and vibe but same amount of fun as always! Make sure to wear your best luau beach attire for this one!

-Registration will be morning of from 9:00am-10:30am
-Pond Skim begins at 11:00am
-Costume contest and pond skim winners announced around 12:30pm
-Live music from Flirtin’ with Disaster from 1:00pm-4:00pm to keep the party going once the pond skim is over.

Free entry for anyone who wants to participate in the pond skim this year, just need to have a Season Pass or Lift Ticket (40% off!).

As always there will be specialized t-shirts for sale, awesome food and drinks like Pina Coladas!

Going to be the Pond Skim and snow beach party of your winter dreams!
