Total Slopes Open: 38

Total Terrain Parks Open: 1 Top of Expo Slope: 1 Rail line, 1 Box line.

Total Glades Open: 8

Conditions: Machine groomed, Packed powder

Temperature: 13°F (-10°c)

Average Base Depth: 30"-50"

  • Easiest
  • More Difficult
  • Black
Weather Forecast

Mostly cloudy. A chance of snow showers in the afternoon. Light west winds. Chance of snow 40 percent.

Caution

Dawn to Dusk Uphill traffic is allowed with your annual $25 Uphill Traffic Pass. If you are skinning uphill with a group on an open ski slope please do so in single file. Please stay away from terrain where we are currently making snow or grooming and look out for our mountain operations crew on the hill.
