Snow Report

Updated Saturday January 31st

New snow last 24 hours: 0″

New snow since last week: 13″

Total snow depth: 2+ feet

Conditions Report

It’s another semi-tropical day at Arctic Valley, with a temperature of 34 degrees (above zero!) at the lodge, and 27 degrees at the top of Chair 2. Winds are light at under 10 mph this morning, and we should have mostly cloudy skies today.

We’ve received about a foot of new snow in the past week, along with a fair bit of wind. The good news is that allowed us to move a bunch of snow around and cover a bunch of bare spots near the top of the T-bar, and increase coverage throughout the groomed areas. Bad news is you’ll need to hunt some of the more protected areas (Shooter, Avalanche Gully, Brandy Bowl) for deeper snow. Reports from Snow Safety yesterday indicate the snow is a bit grabby and will ride better once it’s been chopped up a bit.

Ski & snowboard rentals are available this weekend.

Note for backcountry users: We are closed to inbounds uphill travel. Access to Mt Gordon-Lyon will be through the normal trail, starting from the hiking kiosk in the upper parking lot across from the lodge. New this season, access to Ship Creek will start from the Marston Trailhead (across from the lower parking lot) and continue behind Chair 1. We will have signage and a rope line up this week to mark this route.

Road Conditions

Updated: Saturday January 31st

Arctic Valley Road has been plowed, and we’ll be spreading some sand on the downhill side this morning. The road is in good winter driving condition. We always recommend AWD/4WD vehicles well equipped for winter travel. Please follow the speed limits and drive smart!

Below is the current schedule for military live fire exercises on Arctic Valley Road:

For most exercises, road guards will allow traffic to flow every 20-30 minutes in each direction. For some exercises, the waits may be 30-40 minutes. The road (unless noted above) should not be ‘closed’. If road guards are communicating longer wait times than those listed above, or telling you the road is closed, please call JBER Range Control at 907-384-6230 and report the issue. They will work to make sure the units conducting training are following the road access rules.
