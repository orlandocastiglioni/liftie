Written Report

Today's Hours: 10 a.m.- 6 p.m. (Black Bear & Bear Rug 4 p.m.)

Comments: Update - Another great day to get outside! On the frontside, King Pine, White Pine, Pine Cone, Pine Spills, Scotch Pine, Knotty Pine, East Slope, and both the Bear Rug Carpet and Cubby Carpet Learning Center areas are open. On the backside, Jack Pine, Pine Slalom, Red Pine and Pokey Pine are open. Four lifts are scheduled to open at 10 a.m. including Polar Bear (6 p.m.), Black Bear (4 p.m.), Cubby Carpet (6 p.m.) and Bear Rug (4 p.m.). All trails are scheduled to be groomed nightly and conditions should be ideal machine-groomed packed-powder snow with crisp morning corduroy surfaces. Some areas of ungroomed variable snow may also be encountered due to our snowmaking team bolstering some open trail areas, in addition to trails to open next when conditions permit, including Crooked, Board, Pitch & Slow Pokey. Uphill access for "alpine touring" is allowed in accordance with our Uphill Access Policy.

For the Purity Spring Resort XC & Snowshoe Reserve, Tuesday's new accumulations added to a natural base of 6-12" on XC & Snowshoe trails and a good portion of trails accessible from The Mill parking areas were groomed last Sunday including Mill Connector to Sunset Beach, Enchanted Forest, as well as Brook, Overlook, & Valley trails to and from the Pine Meadows Tubing Park. XC trails North of the lake and along King Pine have not been groomed. Caution, lake crossings are not open. Please note, dogs are not allowed on trails on weekends and a valid ticket or season pass is required at all times to access trails.

Improve your experience on the slopes with our top-notch ski school instructors, including New Women's Academy, Pioneer Race Training & 2-Day Children's Snowboarding Programs. Our rental shop is here to provide you all the skiing, snowboarding, and other winter fun activities equipment you may need, and the tune shop is ready to keep the gear you own sharp and in top shape.
