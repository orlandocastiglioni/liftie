Important Notices

⚫︎Kids Park offers a limited number of sleds and similar items available for free rental. Please note that there are no permanent playground equipment or fixed attractions.

⚫︎Multiple mogul courses are permanently set up within Earth Dragon Field (may be affected by snowfall).

⚫︎Please enjoy the tree run courses at your own risk and with full caution. We recommend wearing safety gear such as helmets and snow protectors.

⚫︎For the safety of all users and facilities within the Togari Onsen Ski Resort managed area, the operation of unmanned aerial vehicles (drones, remote-controlled aircraft, etc.) is strictly prohibited.

⚫︎Please refrain from setting up tents or doing day camps on the slopes.
