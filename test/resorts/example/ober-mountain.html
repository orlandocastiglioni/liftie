Slope Report

Slope + Snow Conditions

Updated: 03/09/2026, 1:20am

The Skiing and Snowboarding season has concluded with the 100th (and final) day of the season having been yesterday, March 8. We want to thank everyone who made it out to enjoy the slopes this season.

We have many exciting projects planned this summer dedicated to improving our ski season next year, including a new trail that will be the longest trail ever at Ober! Other improvements include snowmaking upgrades and new attractions. Stay tuned to our website and social media for the latest updates throughout the Summer!

The Smoky Mountain Bobsled is re-opening this week and snow tubing will continue for the next few weeks until remaining snow levels are depleted. Other activities remain open, including ice skating, ice bumper cars, the Tennessee Flyer Mountain Coaster, the Cloud Catcher Zipline, scenic chairlift rides, and more. Check out our Fun Passes or Adventure Passes for great bundled deals on all activities, now at a lower price!

MTN Map: Our Slopes

Ober Gatlinburg features 10 diverse trails across 38 skiable acres, catering to all skill levels. With a vertical drop of 500 feet and the longest run stretching nearly a mile, the ski area offers a balanced mix of terrain. A dedicated terrain park adds extra excitement for freestyle enthusiasts. The slopes are serviced by a combination of quad, double, and surface lifts, ensuring smooth access throughout the park.
