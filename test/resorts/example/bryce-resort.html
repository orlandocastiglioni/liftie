Mountain & Weather Report
March 15th, 2026

We will CLOSE Monday, 3/16 for rain and RE-OPEN Tuesday, 3/17. We are still holding snow on many of our frontside trails. We currently have Red Eye, Bootlegger, White Lightning, & Locher Bowl to lower Revenuer’s OPEN plus both carpet lifts accessing Little Nip and Bunny Slope. It will be cold for the next week so we will stay open as long as we can! Most other mountains in the region are closing so if you’re itching for some final turns we have you covered.

50% OFF LIFT TICKETS FOR SEASON PASS HOLDERS OF ANY RESORT

If you have a season pass from ANY resort, bring it to our ticket window now through next week and we'll give you half off lift tickets. No pre-booking required.
