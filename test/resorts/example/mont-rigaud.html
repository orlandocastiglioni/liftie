Recognized as « Quebec's Ski Kindergarten », Mont Rigaud is a perfect family resort to learn skiing and snowboarding. Close to Montreal, Mont Rigaud offers a choice of beginner, intermediate and advance ski trails including a snow park. Our Snow School counts more than 100 instructors allowing you to develop your skills in a fun and safe learning environment. Our snowmaking machines allow for optimal day and evening conditions. Our daily or seasonal packages and our equipment rental service allow everyone to enjoy the quality of our facilities safely. Day, evening or all season, plan your visit now!
