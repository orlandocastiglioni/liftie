Snow Report for January 31, 2026 6:00 am
Rise and shine, weekend warriors! ❄️ Dry January is coming to an end, and while the valleys might be flirting with spring, Lost Trail is nowhere close to being done with winter. A fresh 3” this week laid down a smooth, creamy refresh on the groomers we’ve been loving, and base coverage is holding strong. Expect partly cloudy skies today with temps just below freezing to serve up another day of excellent skiing conditions. Spring chores can wait—winter’s still delivering. 😏🎿
