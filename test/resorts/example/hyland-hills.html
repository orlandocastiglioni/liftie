Trail Designations & Difficulty Ratings

Three Rivers Parks promotes responsible ski-area behavior. All of the trails at Hyland Hills Ski Area are designated with the common trail marking symbols found throughout North America.

  • Green circle = easiest
  • Blue square = more difficult
  • Black diamond = most difficult
  • Orange oval = freestyle terrain (may include half-pipes, terrain parks and terrain features)

Your responsibility on the hill is to know your ability and make yourself familiar with ski-area markings. Skiers and snowboarders should begin with the easiest trail and work up, no matter their ability level.
