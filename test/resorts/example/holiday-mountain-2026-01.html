Halloween Party

October 24, 2025

Mark your calendars — you won’t want to miss this one!

Friday, October 24th, we’re turning up the fun at the South Lodge for a Halloween Party like no other! Grab your friends, throw on your best costume, and get ready for a night packed with great music, delicious food, and spooky good vibes.

🍻 Great Local Brews – Featuring your favorite craft beers on tap.
🍔 Delicious Food – Mouthwatering eats fresh from the kitchen.
🎶 Live Music – Our friends Flirtin’ With Disaster will be rocking the house from 7–10pm, bringing all your favorite hits and keeping the energy high all night long!
👻 Costume Contests – Come dressed to impress! Prizes for the best, funniest, and most creative costumes.

The bar and kitchen open at 6pm, the band hits the stage at 7pm, and the party keeps rolling until 11pm. So, dust off your dancing shoes, plan your costume, and get ready to eat, drink, and dance the night away — Holiday Mountain’s Halloween Party is the place to be this spooky season!

Oktoberfest Beer Festival 2025

October 4, 2025

🍺 Oktoberfest 2025 🍺

Get ready for one of the biggest fall celebrations in the Catskills – Holiday Mountain’s Oktoberfest!

Join us for a full day of live music, authentic fun, and plenty of seasonal cheer. From stein-holding contests and craft beer tastings to lawn games like cornhole, there’s something for everyone to enjoy.

🎶 Live Music All Day
Featuring Flirtin’ with Disaster and Cruise Control Band bringing the energy and keeping the party going.

🍻 Beer Tastings & More
Sip your way through an incredible lineup of local craft beers, ciders, and Oktoberfest favorites.

🏔️ NEW This Year – Free Chairlift Rides with your Oktoberfest ticket! Take in the fall foliage and mountain views like never before.

💪 Competitions & Games
Show off your strength in our Stein Holding Contest or your skills in cornhole for bragging rights and prizes.

🎟️ Tickets
Your ticket includes entry to the festival and unlimited chairlift rides. Food, beer, and tastings available for purchase.

⚠️ Please Note: Oktoberfest is a 21+ event.

Don’t wait – this fan-favorite festival sells out quickly every year. Gather your friends, grab your tickets, and come celebrate Oktoberfest the Holiday Mountain way! Click “Buy Tickets” at the top of the page to buy now.

SKI NY Ski & Ride Passport Program Is Here!

January 1, 2024 - March 31, 2024

The 2023-24 Passport Program Is Available @ Holiday Mountain

The SKI NY Passport Program-Kids Ski Free* is back for the 2023-24 Season! This year the pass the pass returns with more ski areas accepting it during holiday periods! Like in the past the Passport is valid seven days a week except for holiday periods at certain ski areas as noted. Visit ISkiNY for more details.
